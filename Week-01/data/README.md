# Week 1 Data

The analytical dataset in this folder is a structured extract of selected five-year normal estimates from official Government of India DES *Agricultural Statistics at a Glance* tables.

Variables include crop, five-year period, area, production and yield.

Official source:
https://desagri.gov.in/document-report/agricultural-statistics-at-a-glance-2024/

The raw publications should be obtained directly from the official DES website.
